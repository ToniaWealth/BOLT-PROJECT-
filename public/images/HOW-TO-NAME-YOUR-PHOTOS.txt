         Use .jpg or .png (lowercase).

TIPS:
- Keep file sizes under 1 MB each (the site will load faster).
- JPG is best for photos. PNG is fine too but larger.
- You don't need to resize — the website crops automatically.
- If a photo is portrait (tall) instead of landscape (wide),
  it will still work, it just gets cropped to fit.
